# Slop Check

- Could this belong to 50 unrelated brands?
- If the logo disappeared, would anything identify it?
- Are choices specific to thesis, audience, place, culture, content or product?
- Did unexplained model defaults enter?
- Are reference surfaces being copied instead of principles?
- Is predictable composition appropriate or merely default?
- Are glow, glass, blobs, pills, oversized radii, generic 3D, meshes, icon circles or stock illustration present without conceptual reason?
- Is everything over-containerized into cards?
- Is every section centered/evenly spaced because the model avoids tension?
- Is decoration compensating for weak art direction?
- Does typography have a point of view?
- Does imagery have defined behavior?
- Is there a memorable recurring rule/artifact/tension/behavior?
- Did polish remove all productive weirdness?

A design need not be weird. It must feel intentional and specific.
