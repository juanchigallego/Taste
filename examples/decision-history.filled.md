# Direction History — Terracotta (Soil Testing Platform)

This is an example of a completed decision history showing how directions evolve through KEEP/REMOVE/PUSH feedback across multiple rounds.

---

## V1 — Lab Coat Precision

**Status:** rejected

**Hypothesis:** Express "scientific agriculture" through the visual language of laboratory equipment: clinical whites, precise measurement graphics, technical diagrams, mono-spaced data displays.

**Worked:**
- Data visualization approach felt credible and expert
- Mono-spaced type for readings reinforced precision
- Users trusted the scientific positioning

**Failed:**
- Felt cold and intimidating—farmers described it as "not for us"
- Clinical aesthetic created distance from the land/soil subject matter
- No warmth or humanity despite the "accessible expertise" thesis

**KEEP:**
- Precision in data display
- Technical credibility signals

**REMOVE:**
- Clinical white palette
- Laboratory/medical visual metaphors
- Sterile photography style

**PUSH:**
- Find expertise signals that don't feel clinical

**Next route:** REFERENCE — need territory for "credible but warm expertise"

---

## V2 — Agronomist's Field Notes

**Status:** superseded (merged into V3)

**Hypothesis:** Express "accessible expertise" through the visual language of a working agronomist's field notebook: handwritten annotations, specimen photography, kraft/paper textures, botanical illustration.

**Worked:**
- Warmth and humanity immediately present
- Connection to soil/land subject matter was strong
- Field photography felt authentic and trustworthy
- Users said "this feels like it's made by people who understand farming"

**Failed:**
- Illustration-heavy approach would be expensive to maintain
- Handwritten elements felt precious/artisanal—wrong register for enterprise sales
- Textures started feeling decorative rather than meaningful
- Risk of "twee farm-to-table" cliché

**KEEP:**
- Specimen-style photography treatment
- Warm, muted earth palette derived from soil types
- Connection to physical land/place

**REMOVE:**
- Handwritten/script typography
- Heavy kraft paper textures
- Botanical illustrations as system elements (OK as occasional editorial)

**PUSH:**
- How can we keep the warmth but feel more systematic/modern?

**Next route:** DIVERGE — hybrid with V1's precision

---

## V3 — Soil Survey Modern

**Status:** selected → CODIFY

**Hypothesis:** Combine V1's scientific precision with V2's earthiness. Express "accessible expertise" through the visual language of modern geological/soil surveys: systematic grids, scientific color coding, specimen photography, technical type with generous spacing.

**Worked:**
- Feels credible AND approachable
- Color system based on actual soil classification (Munsell) creates authentic expertise signal
- Grid-based layouts feel professional without clinical coldness
- Typography (technical grotesque) works for data AND has warmth through generous spacing
- Users said "this looks like real science, but welcoming"

**Failed:**
- Initial attempts at "geological" texture backgrounds felt forced—removed
- Pure Munsell colors too muted for UI affordances—added functional accent

**KEEP:**
- Munsell-derived soil color palette
- Specimen photography treatment (soft light, subtle shadow, cream backgrounds)
- Technical grotesque type (Söhne) with loose tracking
- Grid system based on survey maps
- Data visualization style from V1

**REMOVE:**
- Any remaining artisanal/handmade signals
- Decorative textures

**PUSH:**
- Develop signature artifact: soil core cross-section as recurring graphic element
- Test the system in data-heavy dashboard contexts

**Next route:** CODIFY — formalize into DESIGN.md

## How to use this example

This decision history demonstrates:

1. **Rejection is productive** — V1's failure revealed what "scientific" shouldn't mean
2. **Merging is not collaging** — V3 wasn't "V1 colors + V2 photography" but a new hypothesis
3. **KEEP/REMOVE/PUSH are specific** — not "keep the vibe" but "keep specimen-style photography treatment"
4. **Status tracking** — know what's active, what's dead, what evolved
5. **Routing is explicit** — next phase is named, not implied
